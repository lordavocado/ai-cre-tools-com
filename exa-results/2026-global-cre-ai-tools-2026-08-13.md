## Decision rule

General-purpose AI products qualify only when their official site documents a concrete built-environment workflow.

## Filtering observations

- Accelerator branding is not a quality guarantee. REACH ANZ 2026 was mostly recruitment, CRM, and residential agency software, producing no immediate CRE AI additions.
- Some search results mislabel general AI as real estate. YC's Crow appeared as “AI for Commercial Real Estate” in search, but its first-party profile shows a general chat interface for software products, so it was excluded.
- Construction programs produce many advanced materials, robotics, energy, and industrial-hardware companies. They may be excellent businesses but do not fit this software-directory schema.
- Convergence matters: AutoSitu appeared independently in both YC and Pi Labs, making it one of the strongest accelerator-sourced candidates.
- Regional programs are most valuable for discovery, while YC, MetaProp, and Navitas currently provide the best evidence for product readiness.
